Had a blast speaking at the **Slack Community panel event**! 💬✨

I joined my incredible teammates **Carmen**, **Rie**, and **Meghna** for *“How the Slack Team Uses Slack,”* where we pulled back the curtain on what it’s like to work on the world’s best messaging product. 🔍

As a **Senior Software Engineer** working on the **Slack sidebar** (and previously on the **Lists** feature), I got to share more about:

🌟 **My favorite Slack features** — from custom sections to our secret seasonal easter eggs we only show internally 🎃
🧠 **How we craft delightful frontend experiences** that balance usability with joy
⚡ **Pro tips for using Slack like a power user**, and how to make the most of 700M daily messages
🛠️ **The hidden magic behind features you use every day**, powered by thoughtful design and collaboration

Over the past few years, I’ve learned that community spaces like this one are *so important*—especially when they center genuine connection, learning, and shared passion for what we build. There’s nothing better than connecting with folks who love Slack as much as we do. 💛

Huge thanks to **Jacob** for organizing and to everyone who joined us at **Salesforce Tower** for making it such a fun, energizing evening! 💯

If you want to catch future events, meet Slack engineers, or score some community swag, join the **San Francisco chapter** at [slackcommunity.com](https://slackcommunity.com). 🌉
