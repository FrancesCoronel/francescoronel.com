# These are interesting times.

After applying and interviewing for a bunch of internships and coding bootcamps and receiving a bunch of rejections and acceptances, I’ve decided to head to [Fullstack Academy](https://fullstackacademy.com/) this summer which is located in New York City to be part of their inaugural class for their [Summer of Code](https://www.fullstackacademy.com/summer-of-code) program. Ok, wow, that was a mouthful!
![](https://uploads-ssl.webflow.com/63b0b035359373d0cc473202/63b28eff3214e892f4a6497b_VteEhhF.png)
It’s only for college kiddies and surprisingly there’s not many programs designated specifically for us, so it’s pretty cool that Fullstack decided to implement this.
