## Company

[Signifyd](https://www.signifyd.com/careers/) is an online retail fraud protection company.

## Moderator

[Mariana Ortiz-Reyes](https://www.linkedin.com/in/maortizreyes/)

## Format

October 12, 10 am to 11 am PT

- 45 minutes of fireside chat
- 15 minutes of Q&A

## Key Topics

- Personal Journey
- story of belonging as a Latina woman in tech and specifically in engineering
- how you grew as a leader
- why Hispanic Heritage Month is important to you
- Techqueria
- your work with communities like Techqueria
- Fuego ERG
- your mission as a co-lead of Fuego
- advice for our ERG and for our company as we work towards DEI goals

## Audience

~25 employees were in attendance.

The entire company was invited to join across different time zones (UK, East and West US, Mexico, Brazil).
