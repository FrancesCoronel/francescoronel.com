On **March 20, 2025**, I had the honor of speaking at Ripple’s *Mujeres Fuertes Fireside Chat*, hosted by the Amigues ERG in celebration of **Women’s History Month**. 🎉✨

The panel brought together Latina leaders across fintech and tech—including myself and **Priscila Couto** (Trust & Safety at Google)—to explore how our cultural identities, lived experiences, and personal values have helped us navigate complex systems, build impactful careers, and thrive in Big Tech.

Moderated by **Angel Gutierrez**, the conversation flowed through:

- 💼 **Career journeys and pivots**: the unexpected paths that led us to where we are today
- 🧠 **Transferable superpowers**: resilience, storytelling, adaptability, and self-advocacy
- 🌎 **Latinidad in leadership**: how our heritage and community continue to shape our impact

I shared how I’ve grown from product-focused IC to people-focused EM, what I wish I’d known earlier in my journey, and the industry trends I believe Latinas in tech should be paying close attention to—especially around AI, equity, and visibility.

It was a joy to celebrate *mujer power* in community with Amigues, Women @ Ripple, and allies from around the world. A huge thank you to **Ajshia Najafian** and the Ripple team for the warm welcome, thoughtful planning, and opportunity to uplift Latina voices in tech. 🫶🏽

Let’s keep showing up for each other, *fuertes* as ever.
