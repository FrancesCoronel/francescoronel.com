**I am now co-leading Slack's second-ever Community Incubator Group, Fuego!**

Fuego is Slack's new employee group that will strive to provide visibility, community, and a sense of belonging for the Latinx and Hispanic employees and allies in and outside of Slack.

As Co-Lead of Fuego, my responsibilities include:

∙ Building out the leadership team (treasurer, volunteers, executive sponsor)
∙ Attending DEB Council Meetings
∙ Creating OKRs and managing the budget
∙ Articulating a mission statement tied to business objectives
∙ Driving the yearly planning and community building events
∙ Fielding needs and desires of current and potential members
∙ Defining and implementing metrics that measure our impact
