**I was a judge at the Code Nation 's fourth annual **[**Bay Area Hackathon**](https://codenation.org/code-the-movement/)** on February 7th. **

I had a great time putting on my judging hat and picking the winners for this year's [Code Nation](https://www.linkedin.com/company/5296132/) Hackathon in the Bay Area. 🏆

With the theme being "Code for a Movement", the 1st place team focused on creating a website around education and resources related to foster care. 📝

Shout out to fellow judges John Kodumal and Clarence Wooten for collaborating and a big thank you to Juliana Smith for the opportunity! 💯
