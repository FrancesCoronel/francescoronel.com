I organized a personal branding workshop for members of Slack's Earthtones ERG. Earthtones is the ERG for people of color at Slack.

Mina Markham who is a legend in the tech field was the speaker, and I handled the logistics and marketing.

We had a good turn out but unfortunately, about mid-way through, we got a report that an employee got COVID, and everyone was ordered to evacuate and head home. 

And that was the last time I was in the Slack office.
