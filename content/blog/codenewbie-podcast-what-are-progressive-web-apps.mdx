### What are progressive web apps? And why are they so important? On the 200th episode of CodeNewbie, I break down what progressive web apps are, how they work, and why they make the web more accessible. 🎙️

[CodeNewbie](https://www.codenewbie.org/podcast) features stories from people on their coding journey. Recording was on November 27th, 2018. Released as the 200th episode on February 17th, 2019.“What are progressive web apps? And why are they so important?
