This article revisits four coding bootcamp alumni—Frances Coronel (Fullstack Academy), Pedro Martin (General Assembly), Alon Robinson (Hack Reactor), and Jennifer Sardina (Flatiron School)—to see how their careers have evolved three years after graduation.

Each alum shares their journey, from landing their first jobs to excelling in their fields, along with reflections on skills gained, challenges faced, and advice for aspiring bootcampers. From building confidence in networking to mastering technical skills, these professionals highlight the transformative impact of bootcamps. They also provide insights into thriving in tech, navigating imposter syndrome, and continuously learning new technologies.

The article emphasizes the growing relevance of coding bootcamps as a pathway into tech careers, underscoring their value alongside traditional education.
