As a judge for Technovation 2018, I was responsible for the following:

- Help select 12 finalist teams to compete for scholarships and an opportunity to pitch their idea in front of major tech companies in the Bay Area.
- Assess problem-solving and mobile innovation on a global landscape (more than 1,500 teams from over 110 countries).
- Provide feedback to motivate young girls about their ideas.
- Inspire young women to enter STEM Industries.
