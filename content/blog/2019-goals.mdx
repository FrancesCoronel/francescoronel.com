### Here are some things I'd like to accomplish by the end of 2019.

Here are some things I’d like to accomplish by the end of 2019. A lot of these are already planned but some are stretch goals! Let’s see what happens - but I’ll remind myself in Todoist to look at this post come December 8th, 2019.

 	- Become a senior software engineer 	- Start a podcast (#TechQueens) 	- Read 30+ books 	- Talk about technology or my career at 15+ events (conferences/Meetups/podcasts/etc.) 	- Travel to Hawaii and another place I’ve never been (preferably outside the US) 	- Write 5+ articles that get published outside of my personal website (Medium publication, external blog, etc.)
> These are more long-term or broad goals.
 	- Be loved and love others 😊 	- Give a TED talk 	- Write a book 	- Become a leader at a tech company (VP Eng, Chief Architect, CTO, CEO, etc.) 	- Be on the radio 	- Donate or raise $10K for charity 	- Be involved in the production of a movie I’d actually watch and enjoy 	- Record a song 	- Live in Peru for 6+ months 	- Have the ability to retire earlier but then decide not to because I’d probably get bored 	- Be known as an industry wide expert in something 	- Attend a masquerade ball 	- Own a doggo 	- Try out being a teacher 	- Lead by example and influence a generation of minority women to break into and succeed in tech
