Had such a great time at the **CTOs Dinner** hosted by the team at [Persona](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/edugiansante_personally-persona-cto-activity-7294904656881729537--8ws/) and **AWS**! 💡

It was an intimate evening at *Taksim* in San Francisco filled with thoughtful conversation, laughter, and genuine connection among engineering leaders from companies like Yelp, AngelList, AWS, xAI, PayJoy, and more.

As someone who loves building community and swapping stories about scaling teams and technology, I left feeling inspired and energized. Big thanks to **Ed Giansante**, **Charles Yeh**, and **Liz Nell** for creating such a welcoming space. 🙌🏽

Can’t wait for the next one! 💯
