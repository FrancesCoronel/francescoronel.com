### Women Impact Tech (WIT) is an exclusive event that aligns hundreds of top women engineers with companies who are committed to inclusion and innovation.

🌐 [https://www.andiamogo.com/WITSF](https://www.andiamogo.com/WITSF)

Women Impact Tech (WIT) is an exclusive event that aligns hundreds of top women engineers with companies who are committed to inclusion and innovation. Centered as a celebration of women in tech, attendees will have a chance to network with companies and other female engineers working on cutting edge technology. Through panels, keynotes and presentations, companies and attendees alike will engage in meaningful and actionable conversation surrounding the past and future impact of women in tech.https://www.andiamogo.com/WITSF
