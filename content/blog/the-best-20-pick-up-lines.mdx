### 💘 Pick-Up Lines I Thought Of

I first made this list back in January 2014 — partly for laughs, partly for love. Most of these are cheesy, but hey, maybe I’m just cheesy for you 😉

**1.** (holds out hand) Would you hold this for me while I go for a walk?
**2.** You look cold. Want to use me as a blanket?
**3.** I wanna live in your socks so I can be with you every step of the way.
**4.** There isn’t a word in the dictionary for how good you look.
**5.** Gee, something’s wrong with my phone — it doesn’t have your number in it.
**6.** Kiss me if I’m wrong, but dinosaurs still exist, right?
**7.** Your lips look so lonely… would they like to meet mine?
**8.** Excuse me, I just noticed you noticing me — just wanted you to know I noticed too.
**9.** If I were a transplant surgeon, I’d give you my heart.
**10.** I’m sorry, were you talking to me? No? Well then, please start.
**11.** Was your father an alien? Because there’s nothing else like you on Earth.
**12.** You be the Dairy Queen and I’ll be your Burger King — treat me right and I’ll do it your way.
**13.** You must be Jamaican, because Jamaican me crazy.
**14.** I have an “owie” on my lip. Will you kiss it and make it better?
**15.** On a scale of 1–10, you’re a 9… because I’m the 1 you need.
**16.** If I had a star for every time you brightened my day, I’d have a galaxy in my hand.
**17.** Did you swallow a magnet? Because I can’t pull myself away.
**18.** No matter how much I practice, I’ll never make pictures of you pretty enough.
**19.** A whale’s heart weighs 13,000 lbs — but it still couldn’t love you as much as I do.
**20.** (looks at the leaves) Even the trees are falling for you 🍂
