### A living directory of thriving Latinx software engineers.

Latinxs Who Code is a living directory of thriving Latinxs Software Engineers/Coders/Developers/Programmers/Architects/Hackers. We aim to provide a welcoming space to find outstanding people to follow, look for a mentor, make new friends, or discover talented people to join your team. Latinxs was inspired by Latinxs Who Design.[https://twitter.com/latinxswhocode](https://twitter.com/latinxswhocode)[https://github.com/carlosangon/latinxswhocode.com](https://github.com/carlosangon/latinxswhocode.com)[https://latinxswhocode.io/](https://latinxswhocode.io/)
