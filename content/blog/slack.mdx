## Key Achievements

- Lead engineer for top 3 pages visited by sales leads creators — [Homepage](https://slack.com/), [Contact Sales](https://slack.com/contact-sales?source=fvcproductions) and [Enterprise LP](https://slack.com/enterprise?source=fvcproductions)
- Influenced $15M in pipeline and 7.7M web impressions as lead engineer for [Reinvent Work campaign](https://slack.com/reinvent-work)
- Lead engineer and maintainer for Contact Sales form. - Since launching form in December 2018, there's been a total of over 10K unique leads and over $50M in pipeline generated.
- In 2019, the form [generated](https://slack.engineering/the-unsuspecting-beauty-complexity-of-web-forms/) 31.7% of leads and 40.4% of ACV ($8.2M) for conversational marketing efforts. - From August 2020 to August 2021, there were nearly 500K visits to the form alone with a 99.7% success rate on first submission.
- Launched [Fuego](https://slack.com/blog/collaboration/how-employee-resource-groups-stay-connected-during-remote-work), Slack's first Latinx/Hispanic ERG, as Co-Lead and was awarded merit bonus for organizing 13+ workshops and growing active membership by 4x
- Engineered 10+ high impact landing pages including Pricing, Become a Partner, Spec Conference, Workflow Builder Beta, Team Chat, Slack for Good and University Recruiting
- Recognized with a company award ("Win of the Week") for organizing [React training](https://twitter.com/mjackson/status/1054497245637238784) with the creator of React Router for over 30 senior level+ engineers
- Led one of the largest ERG events for D&I and recruiting called "Turning Failure Into Fuego"
