[Raise for Good](https://www.raiseforgood.com/) is reimagining the success formula for women, leaders of color and purpose-driven companies to drive social change. 

They're a strategy studio possessing deep expertise in strategy, capital raising, branding and social impact for nonprofits, funders and high-growth startups.

I participated in their 2021 spring cohort representing [Techqueria](https://francescoronel.com/2018/04/24/techqueria/).

Their 8-week "Nonprofit Brand, Fundraising and Leadership Bootcamp" Bootcamp delivers:

- Proven strategies to increase ROI
- Personalized 1:1 coaching
- Expert-led workshops on brand marketing, fundraising, and financial sustainability
- Resources and networking opportunities that can help you continue to build on the learnings
- Industry leading speakers and mentors
- Program dates: April 6 - June 1

Program topics center on three areas: brand positioning, nonprofit fundraising, leadership, and organizational and financial sustainability. 

Additionally, bootcamp participants are offered pitch practice sessions, mentor/advisor office hours, and a variety of workshops and community learning sessions such as “Authentic Fundraising,” “Improving Leadership Skills with Improv,” and “Transformative Leadership.”
