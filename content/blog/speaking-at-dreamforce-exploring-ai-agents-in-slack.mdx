Spoke at **Dreamforce** for the first time last week! 🎤

I had the honor of presenting **four sessions** on AI agents in Slack at the **Basecamp Theater** in the **Trailblazer Forest**. 🌲

As someone who works on the **Slack Sidebar Systems** team (yes, that thing you stare at all day!) and previously helped launch **Slack Lists**, which now helps showcase Salesforce data inside of Slack, it was amazing to share how Slack is evolving to become a **powerful product as the conversational home for AI agents.** 💯

💬 **Key themes I covered:**

- How Slack’s **700M daily messages** create the perfect context for smarter AI
- Different **use cases of AI agents** with Salesforce + Slack
- Why Slack is **uniquely positioned** as the best operating system for the *#FutureOfWork*

💡 **Personal takeaways from my first Dreamforce:**

- It was exciting to see all the buzz around **Slackbot AI** — basically ChatGPT inside Slack. I’ve been using it a lot at work, and it’s going to be a **game changer** once it’s fully launched. It’s not just another chatbot — it’s an **AI companion** that understands context and can take action across all your Slack data.
- There’s huge opportunity for **Salesforce customers** to unlock new productivity by connecting CRM data with **conversational workflows.**

Couldn’t be prouder to represent the Slack team. The **future of work is conversational**, and it’s happening now! 💛

P.S. Thank you **Jacob** from Slack Community for helping connect me to this opportunity — and to Slack team for the Slackbot AI swag! 🎁
