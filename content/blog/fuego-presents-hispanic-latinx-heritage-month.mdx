Sep.15th to Oct. 15th is Latinx & Hispanic Heritage Month, a period that recognizes the contributions and influence of Latinx & Hispanic Americans to the history, culture, and achievements of the United States. 
![:tada:](https://ktebrbhzg9wasky1.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/blog/63b28f3b9c26954251e0c1ce_1f389%25402x.png)
At 60 million in the US, Hispanics & Latinx are the largest non-white voting group this year and have been the largest ethnic minority group for the last 20 years. 
![:chart_with_upwards_trend:](https://ktebrbhzg9wasky1.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/blog/63b28f3b9c26950c06e0c1cc_1f4c8%25402x.png)
To celebrate, the Fuego employee resource group at Slack will be hosting a series of fun virtual events throughout the month for employees to participate in and enjoy. 
![:fire:](https://ktebrbhzg9wasky1.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/blog/63b28f3b9c26956d50e0c1cf_1f525%25402x.png)
We’ll kick things off on September 15th with an Opening Ceremony joined by Slack's CEO Stewart who will say a few words. Activities throughout the month will range from intimate cooking experiences to "fuegoside" chats, self-defense classes, dance lessons, and more. 
![:dancer::skin-tone-5:](https://ktebrbhzg9wasky1.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/blog/63b28f3b9c2695343fe0c1d0_1f483-1f3fe%25402x.png)
A huge thank you to the amazing Fuego team who worked hard to make this a reality, y'all the best!
