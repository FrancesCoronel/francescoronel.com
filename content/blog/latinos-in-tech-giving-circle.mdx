**I'm happy to join the Latinos in Tech Giving Circle by the **[**Latino Community Foundation Giving Circle Network**](https://latinocf.org/latino-giving-circle-network/) **and give back to our community!**

The Latino Community Foundation Giving Circle Network is the largest network of Latino philanthropists in California with a movement of leaders ready to take action and invest their time, talent, and resource to lift up Latino leaders and their communities.

As a member of the Latinos in Tech Giving Circle, I help decide where to invest the collective resources of the circle that aligns with our funding priorities: Diversity in Tech, Latino Entrepreneurship, STEM Programs for Latinos, Civic Engagement.

Each member contributes a minimum of $1,000 a year.
