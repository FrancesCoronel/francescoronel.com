## Video Interview

After those two steps, the next step was a video interview with two employees from HUGE. This is the only part that didn’t go well apparently. Lol.

## Lessons Learned

On March 23rd, HUGE rejected me but let me know I was a super strong candidate and yadda. I think HUGE required a lot of time and effort from their applicants. Also, I felt that because I asked a somewhat controversial question during the interview (is it true that HUGE employees can be very cliquish?), I was flagged as not compatible for their culture.
