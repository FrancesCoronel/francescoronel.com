Honored to be featured in **Gregor Ojstersek's Engineering Leadership newsletter** — one of 15 engineers and engineering leaders sharing how we're actually doing AI-assisted engineering in practice. ✍🏽

The piece brings together real perspectives on what AI-assisted workflows look like beyond the hype — how experienced engineers are integrating AI into their daily work, what's working, what isn't, and how the human judgment layer still matters enormously.

One of my favorite takes from the piece: *"The human is better at knowing whether that code should exist at all."* That's the right frame. AI can write the code; knowing what to build and why is still on us.

Gregor's newsletter is one of the best in the business for engineering leadership content — highly recommend subscribing if you're a senior engineer or engineering leader navigating this shift.
