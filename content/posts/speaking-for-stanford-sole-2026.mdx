Had such a great time speaking for **Stanford SOLE** (Society of Latinx Engineers) at Toyon Hall! 🎤

I spoke alongside **Celeste Zepeda** and we covered a lot of ground — my career path, the work we're doing at Slack, and the technical challenges that come with building AI tooling at scale.

The conversation naturally gravitated toward a few big themes:

- **Breaking into industry** — what to prioritize as a student, how to think about your first role, and what "fit" actually means when you're evaluating companies
- **Early career decisions** — the pivots I made, what I'd do differently, and why I think being a generalist early is underrated
- **AI tooling** — how I'm using AI in my day-to-day engineering work and what I think that means for the next generation of engineers entering the field

The Q&A turned into one of those conversations where you can tell folks are genuinely curious — not just going through the motions. That energy makes all the difference. 💛

If you're a student thinking about your path in tech, feel free to reach out. I love these conversations.
