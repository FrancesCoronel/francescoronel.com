## Why We Wanted a Corgi

We'd been talking about getting a dog for a while, and once we committed, we knew we wanted a Pembroke Welsh Corgi. Short legs, big personality, endlessly meme-able — what's not to love?

## Finding a Breeder Through Good Dog

Rather than going in blind, we used [Good Dog](https://www.gooddog.com/) — a platform that vets breeders and rescues to help you avoid the scams and puppy mills that are unfortunately common in the corgi world.

On **July 19, 2020**, we submitted applications to a handful of corgi breeders across the country:

- Corgi Corral
- Whimsical Treasure Corgis
- Let Alone Creek Farm Corgis
- Woodrose Farm
- Cavalier Acres Farm
- Raven Black Corgis
- Mill Creek's Cattle Dogs and Pembroke Welsh Corgis

A week later, on **July 26, 2020**, we heard back from Kristie at **Mill Creek's Corgis**, a Pembroke Welsh Corgi and Australian Cattle Dog breeder in North Carolina. Something about the conversation clicked, and we paid our deposit that same day. As I told Good Dog's team at the time:

> "Yes, I did find a great breeder and paid the deposit via Good Dog. Mills Creek Corgis in North Carolina. I originally had some leads from the Corgi American Association but Good Dog has been such a [huge help]."
