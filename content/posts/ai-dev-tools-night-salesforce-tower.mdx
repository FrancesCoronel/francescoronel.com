Co-organized **AI Dev Tools Night** at Salesforce Tower in San Francisco — a packed evening for developers interested in AI agents, dev tools, and great conversations.

The speaker lineup was fantastic:

- **Beyang Liu** — CTO & Cofounder, Sourcegraph
- **Lindsay Rawitscher** — Senior AI Engineer, Agentforce at Salesforce
- **Sam Bhagwat** — CEO of Mastra (YC W25); formerly Gatsby cofounder
- **Jan Čurn** — Founder, Apify
- **Madison Kanna** (MC) — Senior Software Engineer, Walmart Global Tech

Big thanks to co-organizers **YK Sugi** and **Justin Dorfman** for helping make it happen.

Events like this are why I love the SF tech community — bringing builders together to share what they're working on and where AI tooling is headed.
