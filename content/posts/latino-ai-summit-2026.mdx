Got to speak at **The Latino AI Summit** this year on a topic I care a lot about: **making AI tools work for everyone**, not just people with an ML background. 🎤

Before the talk, I offered up 12 complimentary tickets to my network, because the whole point of the session was that you don't need a machine learning degree to get real value out of today's AI tooling. You need curiosity, and a willingness to try things.

That's really the throughline of how I think about AI in my own work: the barrier to entry keeps dropping, and the people who benefit most are the ones who just start experimenting instead of waiting until they feel "qualified." Watching folks from my network register with zero AI background and show up anyway was the best part of putting this together.

If you missed it, keep an eye out for future cohorts: the Latino AI Summit runs sessions like this specifically to get more of our community building with AI, not just reading about it.
