**Date:** February 12 – 14, 2016
**Role:**

- Participant

### 🧠 About

TreeHacks is Stanford University’s official national hackathon. In 2016, over 670 hackers attended — achieving a 50/50 gender ratio, the first major student-run hackathon to do so.

Our team, **Big Hero 4**, built **Ammalia** (Arabic for *surgery*) — a Leap Motion-assisted app that helps surgeons view and manipulate scans while scrubbed in and sterile.

🏆 **Prizes:**

- Best Social Impact Prize – Sponsored by GoodHacks
- Best Use of Dropbox API – Sponsored by Dropbox

### 💡 Project — *Ammalia. Where surgeons go to explore.*

Ammalia uses the [Leap Motion](https://developer.leapmotion.com/) API and the [Dropbox API](https://www.dropbox.com/developers) to let surgeons analyze medical images (like MRI or CT scans) hands-free in the operating room.

- [View on Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/ammalia)
- [View on my portfolio](https://francescoronel.com/portfolio/ammalia/)

We saw potential for radiology image scrolling and manipulation while scrubbed in — and future use cases include 3D scan interaction and real-time data visualization.

### 🚀 Future Features

- 3D manipulation of MRI and CT scans in real time to assist tumor assessment.
- Radiology and cardiology interventions involving catheter manipulation.
- Integration with patient health records (name, age, background, etc.).

### 🧰 Tools

- [Leap Motion](https://developer.leapmotion.com/) — gesture tracking for pan, zoom, and slide controls without touch.
- [Dropbox API](https://www.dropbox.com/developers) — used for storing and organizing patient image folders.
