### My favorite 20 laws of power.

So if you’re not already familiar with Robert Green’s popular book “[The 48 Laws of Power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_48_Laws_of_Power)” that was published in 1998, I’m going to suggest you read it… right now.

Like any popular book, it has its positive and negative criticisms, but I found it immensely interesting for a self-help book. I felt some of the laws mentioned had some real merit so I decided to go ahead and share some of them to let you decide yourself. 

All credit goes to Robert Greene for the laws. I just included my 20 favorite ones so that if you want to read the book, you can still get surprised. Here they are then…

- Never Outshine The Master
- Be Wary Of Friends For They Will Betray You Faster
- Learn How To Use Enemies For They Have More to Prove
- Conceal Your Emotions
- Always Say Less Than Necessary
- Never Do Yourself What Others Can Do For You
- Use Selective Honesty & Generosity
- Crush Your Rival Completely & Totally
- Be Unpredictable
- Isolation is Dangerous
- Do Not Offend The Wrong Person
- Keep Your Own Hands Clean
- Plan Ahead To The End
- Make Any Accomplishment Seem Effortless
- Play Along
- Discover Weaknesses
- Think Differently, Behave Commonly
- Despise Anything Free
- Respect Tradition While Slowly Implementing Reform
- Adapt and Survive
