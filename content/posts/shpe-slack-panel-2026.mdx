Joined a **SHPE x Slack** panel at Salesforce — and I wanted to take a moment before sharing because many of us participated in the National Shutdown the following Friday for reflection and activism.

This panel went beyond job titles. We talked about **identity, momentum, and what it means to keep going as Latinos in tech** during a moment when our community is navigating a lot — fear, exhaustion, uncertainty around immigration and safety. All of it is valid. All of it was present in the room.

The three pillars I kept coming back to:

- **Building visible impact at Slack** — shipping work that matters and making sure it's seen
- **Investing in community** through Techqueria, Fuego, and professional development pods
- **Staying honest about pivoting, growth, and advocating for advancement** — not performing resilience, but practicing it

Whether you're protesting, building power in tech, or simply enduring: all of it is valid. Professional success can be a form of leverage — to protect families, create opportunities, and show up for others.

Grateful to the fellow panelists who created a space that didn't require anyone to shrink.
