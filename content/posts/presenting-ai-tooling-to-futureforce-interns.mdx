Presented to this year's **Futureforce interns** at Salesforce and Slack on AI tooling, alongside **Ashwin Goel**. 🎉

I opened with a quote from GitHub's COO that's been living rent-free in my head: *"in the AI era, the smartest person in the room is no longer the one with the most answers, but the one most willing to be a student again."* That's the whole talk in one line, honestly.

What I'm most excited about isn't any specific tool — it's watching a generation of engineers who grew up with AI-native workflows as the default, not the add-on. They're not unlearning old habits to adopt this stuff; they're building their instincts on top of it from day one. I genuinely can't wait to see how that changes what "good engineering" looks like over the next few years.

Salesforce is hiring 1,000 new grads and interns this cycle to help build the agentic future — if that's you, the door's open.
