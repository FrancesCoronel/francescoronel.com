On January 16, 2025, we brought the *heat* to Salesforce Tower with a 🔥 **Techqueria x Slack career fireside chat**—a powerful afternoon of storytelling, strategy, and community for Latiné professionals navigating the tech industry.

I had the absolute honor of speaking alongside an all-star panel of Latina leaders:

- **Raquel Vélez** – Sr. Engineering Manager, Slack
- **Monica Scott** – Talent Advisor, Salesforce
- **M. Cristina Pullen** – Sr. Technical Writer, Salesforce (moderator)
- Together, we tackled everything from crafting standout resumes to building resilience in a rapidly evolving, AI-powered job market.

💡 Key themes included:

- Future-proofing your career in tech 🧠
- Navigating transitions and leveling up with intention 🚀
- The power of relationships and community in job searching 🤝

This event was extra meaningful to me—it marked **5 years of working on Slack at Salesforce** and gave me the chance to give back to a community that’s supported me throughout my journey.

We had over 100+ attendees, custom Techqueria/Fuego/Latinoforce swag, and cake pops from a local Latino-owned bakery (yes, they were *muy delicioso*). 🌮🧁🎁

Huge gratitude to our partners in making this event such a success:

- **Fuego ERG at Salesforce**
- **Techqueria SF Bay Area**
- Nelson Bonilla, Veronica Sandoval Guerrero, Tany Rios Castro, and Michele Karakaş-Alarcón

We showed up for each other, celebrated wins, and shared the tools to thrive—not just survive—in this industry.
