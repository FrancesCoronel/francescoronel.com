On July 29th, I had the honor of leading a virtual event with the Slack Community Twin Cities chapter, diving deep into Slack’s latest feature—*Lists*. This was an exciting opportunity to showcase how Lists is transforming the way teams manage projects and collaborate directly within Slack.

The event kicked off with an introduction to Lists, where I shared the inspiration behind the feature and how we aimed to simplify project management. From tracking tasks to automating workflows, Lists brings a new level of efficiency to any team. 

During the live demo, I walked participants through various use cases, showing how customer service, IT, and marketing teams can all benefit from this powerful tool.

One of the highlights was the Q&A session, where I answered questions about integrating Lists into daily workflows and the future roadmap for the product. The energy from the Twin Cities community was incredible, and it was amazing to connect with so many passionate Slack users!

If you couldn’t attend, don’t worry—this is just the beginning of many opportunities to learn about Lists and how it can revolutionize your team's workflow. Stay tuned for more demos and AMAs as we continue to build the future of project management in Slack!
