Beth is the Head of Community at Bevy and CMX.

She launched a course called "The Community-Led Event Program Playbook" which featured event strategy for Techqueria that I shared.

## Course Description

This course is the first step in building a Community-Led Event Program for your organization. It is a practical course to give you the tools and insights you need in order to launch your program. You will build the structure of your program and establish clear goals, you will define the leaders and volunteers of your program, and will develop the application and onboarding processes to get them involved, and you will build out the playbook for your program.
