### VSGC stands for Virginia Space Grant Consortium.

## Name

[VSGC Student Research Conference](https://www.vsgc.odu.edu/src/)

## Organizer

Virginia Space Grant Consortium

## Location

NASA Langley Research Center - Hampton, Virginia

## Date

April 11th, 2016

## Role

STEM Bridge Scholarship Recipient

## About

As part of the STEM Bridge program, scholarship recipients are required to attend an annual research conference at the Langley Research Center in Hampton, VA.I was returning back for my final research conference before graduating from Hampton.

### Awards

- April 2016 - [STEM Bridge Scholarship](https://vsgc.odu.edu/awardees/20152016/)  $1,000 renewable scholarship for being a high-achieving minority student studying STEM
