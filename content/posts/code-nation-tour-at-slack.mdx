I volunteered for Code Nation by hosting a group of ESL students from a local high school in San Francisco I set up a panel of 4 women engineers from Slack and also had them take turns in roundtable sessions with the students. Code Nation brings the tech and education communities together to teach coding in high schools, preparing students for a career in tech.
