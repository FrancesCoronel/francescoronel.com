## Event Description

We understand that networking isn’t at the top of everyone’s to-do list. It can be uncomfortable and depending on your personality — incredibly draining. However, there’s no denying the power a strong professional network can have over your career success. When it’s done well, networking will not only help you create a strong community but will give you a competitive edge throughout every stage of your career.

Join [Slack](https://slack.com) and [Latinas in Tech](https://latinasintech.org) on Thursday, December 2, 2021 as we discuss [the importance of networking](https://latinasintech.org/event/utilizing-your-network-community-for-career-development/) and building a strong community, learn what you can do to improve your networking skills for your career advancement.
