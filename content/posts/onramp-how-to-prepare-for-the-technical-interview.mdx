**Event Description**

How prepared do you feel for your next technical interview? Do you wish you could ask the experts how you can stand out? This is your chance!

Join our panel of talented engineers and advocates to learn more about how to succeed in the technical interview, especially if you're a career changer, bootcamp grad, or self-taught.

**Brian Jenney: **Brian is an Engineering Manager and JavaScript developer who started coding at 30. He has taught at several bootcamps and currently mentors developers in the beginning stages of their career looking to accelerate their trajectory and avoid all the mistakes he made in the past.

**James Quick: **James is a JavaScript Developer, Speaker, and Teacher with a passion for teaching developers through content. He is a full-time Technical Content Creator with years of experience in Software Development and Developer Advocacy.

**Frances Coronel: **Frances is a senior software engineer, a fierce advocate for diversity in tech, and a doting Corgi mom. You can find more information about Frances' work at https://francescoronel.com/about/.

**Janelle Leuthaeuser: **Janelle is an Expert Data Analyst at Electronic Arts who has spent the last 6 years analyzing how people play some of the most popular video games in the world. She had a unique journey to data analytics, trying out a variety of STEM fields before finding her passion in data. Her outreach is focused on fostering an inclusive culture and advocating for diverse hiring and promoting, as well as mentorship and development of junior analysts.
